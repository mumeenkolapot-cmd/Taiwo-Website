About Our Company

Nigeria's Trusted Name in Palm Oil Production

Taiwo Integrated Resources Limited operates a fully integrated palm oil value chain. From our extensive plantations in Nigeria's fertile regions to our state-of-the-art processing mills, we ensure the highest quality standards at every stage.

We specialize in producing Crude Palm Oil (CPO), Palm Kernel Oil (PKO), and various palm oil derivatives for international markets. Our commitment to sustainable practices and community development sets us apart in the global agro-commodity sector.

Our Operations

Integrated Value Chain

From seedling to shipping, we control every aspect of production to guarantee quality and traceability.

Plantation

5,000+ hectares of prime oil palm plantations using high-yielding Tenera variety seedlings and modern agricultural practices.

  • Sustainable farming
  • High yield per hectare
  • Traceable sourcing

Processing Mill

State-of-the-art milling facility with 60 tonnes/hour capacity, featuring sterilization, threshing, and clarification systems.

  • High extraction rate
  • Quality control lab
  • Waste recycling

Export & Trading

Direct export capabilities to Europe, Asia, and Americas. ISO-certified storage and handling facilities at Lagos Port.

  • Bulk shipping
  • Containerized exports
  • Documentation support
